Estimate uses model volume, infill and material density. Final quote confirmed by a technician before printing, usually within the hour. Delivery quoted separately by ZIP.
What file should I send?

STL is the universal format and gets measured instantly in your browser. 3MF, STEP and OBJ are fine too; we measure those after upload. If you only have a sketch or a broken part, skip the upload and describe it in the notes.

How accurate is the instant estimate?

Usually within 10–15% of the final price. Overhangs that need support, very thin walls, or exotic colours can shift it. A technician reviews every file and confirms the price before anything runs.

Do you deliver outside Florida?

Yes. Everything is printed in Margate, FL. Local orders can be picked up free or couriered within Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach. We ship tracked to all 50 states.

Can you keep my design confidential?

Yes. Files are stored privately, never published, and deleted on request once the job is complete. NDAs are welcome for commercial work.
